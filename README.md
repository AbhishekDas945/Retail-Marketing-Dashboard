# 🛒 Retail Marketing Dashboard | Power BI & Power Query

📌 Project Overview
The Retail Marketing Dashboard provides an end-to-end overview of sales performance, customer demographics, product categories, and store-level profitability. Using Power Query for data cleaning, shaping, and transformation, along with Power BI for interactive visual storytelling, this project transforms raw retail transaction records into actionable business intelligence.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
Microsoft Power BI Desktop: Interactive dashboard design, data modeling, and visual analytics. 

Power Query Editor: ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) processes, data cleaning, column splitting, data type standardization, and query merging.

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Custom calculated measures for Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, and Sales Growth %.

🔍 Dashboard Features & Key Insights
Dynamic Slicers & Filtering: Equipped with top-level multi-select dropdowns for Year, Month Name, Region, Payment Mode, and Store Type to slice performance metrics in real time.

Monthly Sales Trend: Revenue peaks in January (₹1.70bn) and May (₹1.70bn), maintains strong momentum through the first half of the year (₹1.46bn–₹1.68bn), and stabilizes between ₹1.32bn and ₹1.35bn from July through December.

Category-Wise Performance: Grocery leads revenue generation at ₹4.4bn, followed by Electronics (₹3.5bn), Personal Care (₹2.7bn), Home & Kitchen (₹2.7bn), and Fashion (₹1.8bn). 

Customer Segmentation: The Consumer segment drives the majority of revenue at 70.03% of total sales (₹17.76bn), followed by Corporate (19.94%) and Home Office (10.03%).  

Top 10 Performing Brands: FMCG and household giants dominate brand sales, led by Amul (₹1.17bn), Tata (₹1.15bn), Britannia (₹1.06bn), and Nestle (₹1.06bn), alongside strong apparel and cookware contributions from Puma (₹0.78bn), Adidas (₹0.73bn), Nike (₹0.70bn), Prestige (₹0.62bn), Patanjali (₹0.60bn), and Dove (₹0.56bn).

Top 10 Stores: Store-level revenue is tightly competitive at the top, led by Kolkata Mini Store 19 (₹366.70M), Ahmedabad Mini Store 10 (₹366.69M), Lucknow Mini Store 38 (₹365.62M), Surat Mini Store 18 (₹364.61M), and Jaipur Hypermarket 17 (₹362.30M).

Geographic & Granular Drill-Down: Includes an interactive Sales by Region map across India and a detailed Sales Details matrix tracking individual Sales ID, Customer Name, Membership Type (Gold, Silver), Category, Sub Category, Product Name, Store Name, Sum of Quantity, Total Sales, and Total Profit.
